The World-Wide Web

The official description of the World-Wide Web (WWW.W3) is a "wide-area hypermedia information retrieval initiative aiming to give universal access to a large universe of documents". It is a way of viewing all the on-line informatin avaliable on the Internet as a seamless, browsable continuum.
